Sri Lanka's Premier P2P Equipment Rental Platform

Rentify LK is a peer-to-peer rental platform that connects equipment owners with people who need to rent high-quality tools, electronics, and gear across Sri Lanka.

Our mission is to make equipment rental accessible, affordable, and trustworthy for everyone. Whether you need a camera for a photoshoot, power tools for a project, or a laptop for work, Rentify LK helps you find the right equipment from verified owners near you.

With secure Google authentication, real-time chat, and a user-friendly interface, we're building a community where sharing resources is safe and convenient.
